## When to Use Zoom vs. Sharpening

| Goal | Recommended action |
|------|-------------------|
| Read tiny text or serial numbers | Zoom to 8×–32×, pan to the label |
| Check screenshot UI alignment | Zoom to 200%–400%, use mini-map to jump between panels |
| Recover soft focus or mild blur | Enable Adaptive sharpening at 30–50% |
| Enhance edges in product photography | Try Unsharp Mask at 40–60% |
| Inspect compression artifacts | Zoom to pixel level without sharpening |

Remember: sharpening **enhances edges** — it cannot recover true motion blur or a completely missed focus. Use moderate values for natural results.
